# Walkthrough: Database-Driven Plate Categories & Certified Supervisor Selection

## Overview
We expanded and transitioned plate classifications and supervising certification officers into **fully managed database entities with complete CRUD capabilities**, interactive Active/Inactive toggle switches, automatic badge number generation, and deep integration with the live Ghana Digital Plate visualizer.

---

## 1. Database-Driven Plate Classifications (`PlateCategory`)

### Complete Ghanaian Category Registry in PostgreSQL
All official vehicle plate classifications are stored in the database with custom visualizer tokens:
1. **`PRIVATE`**: Standard White retroreflective plate with black embossing.
2. **`COMMERCIAL`**: Yellow retroreflective plate with black embossing.
3. **`MOTORCYCLE`**: Light blue 2-tier square plate for private & commercial motorbikes / tricycles.
4. **`GOVERNMENT`**: Yellow split GV block with national security flag triangles.
5. **`ELECTRIC`**: Green EV split block on white reflective plate for zero-emission electric vehicles.
6. **`TRAILER`**: Yellow T split block on white plate for haulage, tankers and articulated trailers.
7. **`TEMPORARY`**: Cyan-blue band and embossed TMP serial sticker plate.
8. **`AGRICULTURAL`**: Green AG split block plate for tractors and farm machinery.
9. **`DIPLOMATIC`**: Crimson red reflective plate with white embossing for embassies and diplomatic missions.

### Admin Active / Inactive Switch Controls
- Administrators can visit [`/dashboard/plates/categories`](file:///c:/Users/pasca/OneDrive/Desktop/dvla-vpms/app/dashboard/plates/categories/page.tsx) to view all categories.
- Toggling a category to **Inactive** immediately hides it from the officer's booking selection.
- Toggling to **Active** immediately enables it on the booking desk.
- Live `DigitalPlate` visualizer previews render the authentic Ghanaian plate for every category right on its card.

## 2. Certified Supervising Officers Registry (`Supervisor`)

### Streamlined Data Model (Phone & Email Removed, Auto-Generated Badges)
- **Email and Phone Numbers Removed**: The database schema and UI were simplified to eliminate unnecessary contact fields from the `Supervisor` model.
- **Auto-Generated Badge Numbers**:
  - Badge numbers are now automatically generated by the database upon registration (e.g. `SUP-XXXX`).
  - Officers and administrators no longer need to type badge numbers manually.
- **Select Dropdown (No Free-Text Typing)**:
  - In Step 3 (*Customs & Certification*), the **Supervising Certification Officer** field is a secure `<select id="input-supervisor">` dropdown.
  - Displays certified officers with auto-generated badge numbers and assigned stations:
    - `SAVIOUR ADOM (SUP-0491) — DVLA ADENTA`
    - `OFFICER MENSAH (SUP-0182) — DVLA ADENTA`
    - `PASCAL SETH (SUP-0077) — DVLA HQ`
    - `KWAME OSEI (SUP-0314) — DVLA 37 STATION`
  - Includes an inline **+ Add** quick registration modal directly on the booking desk so officers can register new personnel on the fly.
  - Dedicated management portal available at [`/dashboard/supervisors`](file:///c:/Users/pasca/OneDrive/Desktop/dvla-vpms/app/dashboard/supervisors/page.tsx) with active/inactive toggles.

## 3. Visualizer Integration (`DigitalPlate.tsx`)

The visualizer fully renders all categories with authentic Ghanaian aesthetics:
- **Commercial**: Yellow reflective background gradient (`#ffe066` to `#fcc419`).
- **Motorcycle**: 2-tier stacked square layout (`#35baf6`).
- **Government**: Yellow split block with "GV" and red/green security triangles.
- **Electric**: Emerald green split block with "EV" in white font.
- **Trailer**: Yellow split block with "T" in black font.
- **Agricultural**: Forest green split block (`#15803d`) with "AG" in white font.
- **Diplomatic**: Crimson red metallic background (`#991b1b` to `#7f1d1d`) with white split "CD" badge and white embossed registration characters.
- **Temporary**: Blue sidebar with renewal hole, black horizontal strip with TMP serial, VIN, barcode, and expiration badge.
